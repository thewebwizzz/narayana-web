Rules & Policies

General Guidelines

  • Each school must be represented by two students and one Teacher-in-Charge, who must remain present throughout both days.
  • Attendance at the inauguration ceremony is compulsory for all school representatives.
  • The school shall be responsible for the discipline, conduct, and punctuality of its participants throughout the event.
  • Registration will be conducted on both event days from 8:30 AM to 9:30 AM.
  • All participants must carry a valid school ID card with them.
  • All participants must be registered through the official website or designated school email within the specified deadline. Any changes to participant details must be communicated via email at least 24 hours before the respective event.
  • Unless specified otherwise, participants must remain in their school uniform throughout the event.
  • Participation is open to students of Classes 9–12 only.
  • Right of admission is reserved.
  • The decisions of the judges and referees shall be final and non-negotiable.
  • Any media tracks required for an event must be uploaded to the Google Drive link provided.
  • Schools are expected to reach the venue at least 10 minutes before the scheduled beginning of each event.
  • Political, caste-based, or religion-based remarks, along with vulgar or inappropriate themes, are strictly prohibited during all events.
  • Any instance of cheating, misconduct, misbehaviour, or violence will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Any damage caused to school property will be reported and appropriate action will be taken.
  • Mobile phones must be used responsibly and appropriately. Defamatory posts, comments, or inappropriate conduct may lead to disqualification.
  • Participants are required to cooperate with security personnel and volunteers and follow their instructions at all times.
  • Any suspicious activity should be reported immediately to the event security team.
  • Possession of intoxicants or prohibited/contraband items will result in immediate disqualification and removal from the school premises.
  • All participants are expected to maintain cleanliness and dispose of waste only in the bins provided.
