Privacy Information

Narayana Newtown respects the privacy of all participants, teachers, schools, and visitors using this website.

Information Collected

Registration may include information such as participant name, school name, class, Teacher-in-Charge details, contact information, and registration details.

How Information Is Used

The information collected may be used to process registrations, verify participant and school details, manage activities, and communicate important announcements, schedules, and updates.

Who Can Access Information

Registration information will only be accessible to authorized organizers, school representatives, staff, or other personnel when required for administrative or organizational purposes.

Student Privacy

Only information necessary for registration and administration should be provided. Participants should not submit unnecessary sensitive or personal information through the website.

Photos and Videos

Photographs and videos may be taken during activities for documentation, reporting, or promotional purposes, subject to applicable permissions and requirements of Narayana Newtown.

Data Security

Narayana Newtown will take reasonable measures to protect the information collected through the website from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

Data Retention

Registration and related information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for administrative purposes and maintaining records. Information that is no longer required may be deleted.
